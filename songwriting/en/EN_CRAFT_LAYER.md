## 0. When to open

- Any **EN** GENERATE / AUDIT / REPAIR

- When EN feels "fluent but dead" (AI-default)

**Do not open for:** full craft theory (hub + overlay), RU coldwave desk, mix/master numbers.

## 1. Desk order (EN)

1. Voice + place + **one concrete object**
2. Cost-in-frame (what is lost and still visible)
3. Hook: 1–2 punch lines, say aloud at target BPM
4. Draft
5. Anti-slop bank (§2) pass
6. Detector 2.0 + EN delta (§3)
7. Genre satellite if any (danger levers for IND)
8. PACKAGE → gen

Naturalness test (EN-strict): *Would a person say this out loud — and can they sing it at tempo without chewing consonants?*

## 2. Anti-slop bank (EN)

Flag and REPAIR if density is high (≥2–3 hits across banks, or 1 chorus built on cliché pairs).

### 2.1 Therapy / consoling
`heal, healing, trauma, boundaries, valid, journey, self-care, inner child, hold space, still love, I'm sorry, please stay, almost, maybe` in **outro** especially — outro must not comfort.

### 2.2 Pop-AI glue
`in the end`, `nothing lasts`, `lost in the`, `screaming into`, `echoes of`, `broken pieces`, `drowning in`, `fade to black`, `demons in my`, `voice inside my head`

### 2.3 Cheap rhyme pairs (default ban unless ironic frame is explicit)
`night/light`, `heart/apart`, `fire/desire`, `pain/rain`, `eyes/lies`, `true/you`, `alone/home`, `scar/are`, `breath/death`

### 2.4 Industrial / cyber stamps (genre_autopilot EN)
`neon veins`, `chrome heart`, `digital god`, `glitch in my soul`, `cyber tears`, `binary love`, `system override` as emotion, `jacked into`

### 2.5 Syntax anti-patterns (P10)
- `It's not X, it's Y` / `I'm not X, I'm Y`
- Hypophora (ask → answer yourself)
- Uniform line length + parallel stacks with no turn
- Abstract pile: *shadows, pain, void, silence* with no object

### 2.6 Legal craft (do not false-flag)
- Production lexicon with **dual-read** (literal desk/gear + human mask)
- Max **1** denial line per lyric — legal only when the hero denies their own **inner state / intent** ("Not grieving — pulling"); if the construction describes properties of an object / the world / the mix, it is not_x_but_y (banned, §2.5). Unsure → REPAIR
- One concrete remainder after a cut (empty cup, dead LED, phone face-down)
- Second person that **acts** at least once (from IND-002+ levers)

## 4. Prosody & hook (practical)

- **Stress:** speak the line; primary stress lands on meaning words, not particles.
- **Multisyllable crush:** `temperature`, `vulnerability`, `definitely` — usually bad in chorus; break or swap.
- **Hook test:** chorus must land in one breath-group; 1–2 lines people can bark back.
- **Register lock:** clinic / street / literary — pick one per section; don't therapy-climb in the last four bars.
- **Title line:** if the title is in the chorus, it carries weight — no filler around it.

## 5. EN GENERATE micro-scaffold

```
VOICE: who speaks + how calm/cruel
PLACE: one room or street fact
OBJECT: touches or operates (fader, cup, latch, meter)
COST: what warm thing is gone + visible remainder
YOU: does "you" act once? (required IND-002+)
HOOK: two lines max
OUTRO: fact or action — no comfort lexicon
ANTI-SLOP: scan §2
```
