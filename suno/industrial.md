## Vocal anchors

- Default: Deep husky dry close-mic
- Bridge alt: almost-whisper over bare pulse (still dry — no cathedral)
- Never: female default, belting peak, scream lead (unless explicit experiment card)

## Negatives

`no organ, no cathedral reverb, no piano, no flute, no epic choir, no house four-on-the-floor, no polish, no screaming, no shouting, no wailing, no humming, no vocal acrobatics, no vocals during intro`

## MIX quick (sub-king)

- Sub: king, mono to ~120 Hz; HP everything else below that band
- Sidechain: hard, fast attack, ~1/8 release @ 140
- Dry: reverb ≈ 0; room tail only on foley
- Growls: sub mono low / mid-growl stereo split
- EQ: do **not** auto-port coldwave stack (+4 @ 3k). IND gens often harsh 2–5k — hypothesis; confirm/drop by first-gen measure
- Master Spotify: LUFS −14 · peak −1.5…−2.0 · crest 10–12
- Club option: −9…−8 LUFS, crest 6–7 (conscious trade)

## Common breaks → fix

| Break | Fix |
